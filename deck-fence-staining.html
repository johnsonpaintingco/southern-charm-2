Our approach

Deck & Fence Staining planned around your property

Every project is different. We want to understand the scope, your expectations, and preparation concerns before confirming the work and schedule.

Evaluate the wood first

Wood species, age, previous coatings, moisture, and sun exposure affect preparation and finish selection.

Prepare according to the product

Cleaning, drying, sanding, and removing failed coatings may be needed depending on the wood and manufacturer's directions.

Select the right appearance

Transparent, semi-transparent, and solid-color finishes have different looks and maintenance needs.
